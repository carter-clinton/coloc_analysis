## Why the 4 destructive grants were refused

The four entries removed before staging were:

```
Bash(rm -rf data/processed/clumping/*/ data/processed/clumping/.m2_clumping_complete)
Bash(rm -f logs/m2_04_clumping.fire.log)
Bash(PARALLEL=20 nohup bash bin/fire_m2_04_clumping.sh)
Bash(echo "Fired PID $!")
```

They were transient grants for a single M2 clumping fire that has **already run**. An entry in `permissions.allow` is a **permanent privilege grant** — it executes in every future session with **no prompt**. Committing them would have permanently pre-approved (a) a recursive delete under `data/processed/` and (b) an unattended 20-way background compute launch. Persisting a standing `rm -rf` over the *same tree* whose accidental deletion this task repairs — **in the same task** — is self-defeating. The cost of removal is one re-prompt if that fire is ever re-run.
