### Lead-variant extraction logic (Panel A input)

For each non-empty `results/fine_mapping/susie/*.json` (n = 51 fits with at
least one non-null credible set), enumerate variants across all credible
sets and pick the single top-PIP variant (representative lead for that
region × trait × ancestry fit). Retain (chr, pos, pip, cs_name) per lead.

### Classification proxy (Panel A)

For each lead (chr, pos), intersect against MAGMA's NCBI37.3 gene loc:
- "Within gene body" if pos lies in [start, end] of any gene on same chromosome
- "Flanking ≤100 kb" if closest gene boundary within 100 kb (but not within gene body)
- "Distal intergenic" otherwise

This is a CONSERVATIVE PROXY for coding/regulatory; true coding-variant
classification requires exon-level annotation (not just gene boundaries)
+ CADD/PolyPhen/SIFT functional scoring (not on disk). The in-figure
caption states this explicitly.
