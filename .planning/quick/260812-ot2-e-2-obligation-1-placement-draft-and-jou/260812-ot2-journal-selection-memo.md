## 3. The ladder (the recommendation)

**Nature (flagship) → [optional single rung: Nature Genetics] → Nature
Communications → AJHG / Genome Biology → Genome Medicine → PLOS Genetics /
HGG Advances.**

1. **Nature (flagship) — first, per the directive.** Expect a fast desk
   reject (usually days–2 weeks; model knowledge — verify). The real value of
   the rung: satisfying the directive costs near zero, and a Nature decision
   opens the **formal Manuscript Transfer Service** — editor destination
   referral in the decision letter, optional referee-report transfer
   (verified 2026-08-12).
2. **Nature Genetics — optional single rung.** Low odds: it publishes
   statistical-genetics pitfall papers but expects field-scale evidence, and
   a 10-admissible-region EUR core is a likely desk reject; slightly better
   than the flagship because the LD/coloc topic sits squarely in its methods
   lane. Skippable without losing the cascade.
3. **Nature Communications — the realistic Nature-brand landing.** OA
   multidisciplinary; publishes rigorous corrective/benchmark genomics;
   receives Nature transfers **with referee reports** (verified 2026-08-12).
   Scale is the main referee risk; the pre-registration + negative-control
   design helps.
4. **AJHG / Genome Biology — the two STRONG-fit rungs.** AJHG: ASHG flagship,
   fine-mapping/coloc methodology critiques and ancestry-equity papers are
   its natural register. Genome Biology: methods/benchmarking audits are its
   core identity and its reviewer pool actually reads LD-methods arguments.
5. **Genome Medicine — the incumbent.** Fully viable; ranked below Genome
   Biology on methods-nativeness; translational hook = drug-target
   implications of inflated coloc claims + the health-equity angle.
6. **PLOS Genetics / HGG Advances — the high-probability floor.** Both
   explicitly hospitable to rigorous corrective work of this scope.

- **eLife — a parallel-philosophy option, NOT a rung.** Maximal
  rigor-transparency culture and negative/corrective work is explicitly
  welcome, but since the 2023 model change there is **no accept/reject gate**
  (output = Reviewed Preprint + public assessment) and it has **no current
  Journal Impact Factor** following the Web of Science/JCR break (verified
  2026-08-12) — a real cost against the "most reputable" criterion for a
  solo-author career artifact.
- **Nature Metabolism — pruned.** Weakest scope fit on the list: the journal
  centers on metabolic mechanism/physiology, and an LD-methods audit is
  peripheral there.

**Honest top-3 by reputation-per-realistic-odds** — stated even where it cuts
against the Nature-first aim; Carter said "we can adjust for others":

1. **AJHG** — the field's natural home for statistical-genetics audits, with
   ancestry equity a core editorial theme; solo-author methods-rigor papers
   are within its normal register; hybrid model gives a $0 route. Best
   reputation-per-realistic-odds.
2. **Nature Communications** — the highest-reputation venue on the list with
   a genuine (not flattering) chance of acceptance for corrective work at
   this scale, and the natural landing of the Nature-first cascade — the
   ladder costs one desk-reject cycle, not a restart. APC $7,350 is the price
   of the brand (verified 2026-08-12).
3. **Genome Biology** — its genomics-methods reputation is arguably second
   only to the Nature titles for this specific paper genre; the equity angle
   lands as pipeline-generalizability (Track B AoU-AFR panel), which matches
   this journal's register.
