## Lessons

1. **A scope assertion must name its SUBJECT, not its blast radius.** `260805-w7u` was
   right that *it* must not touch `finemap.smk`; it expressed that as "`finemap.smk` may
   never change again", against a fixed SHA. The very next task's mandated,
   frozen-file-avoiding fix could not land. `diff == ""` is never a contract about
   correctness — it is a countdown to the next legitimate edit. **This is the seventh
   assertion in this arc found structurally incapable of doing its stated job, and the
   first one we wrote ourselves.**
2. **Deleting the orphan would have deepened the silence.** The intuitive fix for finding G
   — remove the chain entry whose artifact nothing builds — leaves TRANS with no AoU entry
   at all, so `strict_aou_only` becomes structurally incapable of ever flagging it. The
   counter-intuitive move (keep it, teach the guard to see it) is the one that closes the
   finding, and it now has its own test.
3. **Build the fixture from a literal, never from the predicate under test.** The
   invariance trees are constructed from a hard-coded `SHIPPED_AOU_SOURCES` set. Had they
   used `is_aou_source`, NC-G1 and NC-G2 would have *moved the fixture along with the
   predicate* and both controls could have passed while broken.
4. **Prove the direction nobody tests.** NC-G2 (`is_aou_source -> True`) is the control
   that matters operationally: an over-firing guard does not produce a wrong number, it
   produces *no fire at all*. It took one line to write and it is the only evidence that
   the fix is safe to turn on.
5. **A one-liner in a Snakemake `shell:` block is a template, not code.** The plan's set
   literal was correct Python and unusable here. Extracting the program and `compile()`ing
   it in a test caught it in seconds; reading it would not have.
6. **"All the sites are in the frozen file" survived an incomplete enumeration — the
   minimal diff would not have.** The plan named 3 of 6 `variant_catalog_fallback` sites.
   The conclusion was still right, but writing K-1's diff against the incomplete list would
   have deleted the wrong line or missed that `:916` must stay.
7. **A narrowed assertion is only stronger if you have watched it fire.** `AUTH-b77-01`'s
   replacement could easily have been a quiet weakening. The control that shadows
   `params.region_id` in a throwaway repo — and separately requires the REAL diff to be
   NON-EMPTY, so its passing half is not vacuous — is what makes "strictly stronger" a
   measurement rather than a claim.
