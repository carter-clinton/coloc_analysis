- **Upper sub-panel**: `geom_point(x = POS, y = -log10(P))` for all variants in the SuSiE-fit-admissible region window, colored by credible-set membership (CS1/CS2/.../NA), with `ggrepel::geom_text_repel` labels on the top PIP variant per CS. Shape or alpha separates in-CS from non-CS variants.
