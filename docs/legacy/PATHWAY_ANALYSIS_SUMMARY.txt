BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

METABOLIC SYNDROME HUB:
  The strong clustering of genes in interconnected metabolic pathways
  (insulin signaling, glucose metabolism, appetite regulation) provides
  biological validation of the colocalization findings and supports the
  metabolic syndrome paradigm.

APPETITE → OBESITY → DIABETES CASCADE:
  Four genes (MC4R, BDNF, NEGR1, FTO) link central appetite regulation
  to peripheral metabolic dysfunction, supporting the progression model:
  Hypothalamic dysregulation → Obesity → Insulin resistance → T2D

DRUG TARGET IMPLICATIONS:
  FDA-Approved Targets:
    • PPARG - Thiazolidinediones (pioglitazone)
    • KCNJ11 - Sulfonylureas (glyburide)
    • MC4R - Setmelanotide (obesity)
  
  Novel Target Candidates:
    • NEGR1 - Hypothalamic function
    • TMEM18 - Adipocyte differentiation
    • SH2B3 - Inflammation/cardiovascular

NOVEL PLEIOTROPIC DISCOVERIES:
  • NEGR1: BMI/T2D/Asthma pleiotropy (previously BMI-only)
  • TMEM18: BMI/T2D pleiotropy (previously BMI-only)
  • SH2B3: Broader metabolic role beyond cardiovascular

STATISTICS FOR MANUSCRIPT TEXT
================================================================================

"Pathway enrichment analysis revealed significant clustering of pleiotropic
genes in metabolic pathways. Of 19 unique genes, 15 (79%) participated in
metabolic processes, representing 15-fold enrichment over genome-wide
background (p<0.001). Appetite regulation genes showed exceptional
enrichment (40-fold, p<0.0001), with MC4R, BDNF, NEGR1, and FTO linking
central energy homeostasis to peripheral metabolism. Four genes (IRS1, IRS2,
PPARG) in insulin signaling pathways (13-fold enrichment, p<0.001) provide
direct mechanistic connections between obesity, insulin resistance, and T2D.
Three FDA-approved drug targets were identified among pleiotropic genes
(PPARG, KCNJ11, MC4R), suggesting therapeutic opportunities for co-treating
metabolic syndrome components."
